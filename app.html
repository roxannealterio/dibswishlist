Questions

Is Dibs Wishlist free?

Yes. Saving from any store, 3 wishlists, sharing 1 wishlist, price checks and one Secret Santa group are free. Dibs+ adds price drop alerts while the app is closed, sharing every list, unlimited lists and full price history.

Which stores does Dibs work with?

Any online store, including TikTok Shop, Instagram, Pinterest, Ticketek and Ticketmaster. Tap Share in Safari or any app, or browse a store inside Dibs and tap Save.

How do price drop alerts work?

Dibs re-checks the real store price of everything you save each time you open it, and every day with Dibs+. Set the price you'd pay and Dibs tells you when it drops, which is perfect for Black Friday, Boxing Day and EOFY sales.

Can I make a wedding, baby shower or birthday registry?

Yes. Make a list for any occasion and share the link. Guests tap I'll get this, or chip in together on a bigger gift, and other guests see what's taken so nothing doubles up. You never see who's getting what, so it stays a surprise.

Does Dibs do Secret Santa?

Yes. Dibs has a free Secret Santa generator: draw names, send everyone a private link, and each person shares a Secret Santa wishlist with the group. It works for family Christmas and work Kris Kringle.

Is there a Dibs app for iPhone?

Yes. The Dibs Wishlist iPhone app launches on the App Store in November 2026. Join the waitlist on the website and we’ll email you on launch day.

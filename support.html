Common questions

How do I save something?

In any app, tap Share and choose Dibs. Or open the Save tab, tap a store, find what you love and tap ♥ Save to Dibs.

How do I cancel Dibs+?

Dibs+ is bought and managed in the iPhone app through Apple. To cancel, go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Dibs Wishlist.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile › Settings › Delete account. This removes your data.
